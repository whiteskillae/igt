Privacy Policy

IBREU Global Talent

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

IBREU Global Talent respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of candidates, clients, employers and visitors who interact with our website, recruitment services, advertisements and digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information including:

  • Full name
  • Phone number and WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Current country or location
  • Educational qualifications
  • Professional experience
  • Job preferences
  • CV or resume information
  • Professional licences and eligibility status
  • DHA, DOH, MOH, HAAD, DataFlow or similar licensing information
  • Interview and recruitment-related information
  • Information submitted through Meta, Facebook, Instagram or other lead forms

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Review your eligibility for employment opportunities
  • Match your profile with suitable vacancies
  • Contact you regarding applications and opportunities
  • Coordinate interviews with potential employers
  • Provide recruitment and placement support
  • Provide licensing, training or documentation assistance where applicable
  • Schedule interviews and recruitment processes
  • Communicate updates regarding your application
  • Improve our recruitment and candidate-support services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. Sharing of Candidate Information

Where necessary for recruitment purposes, IBREU Global Talent may share relevant candidate information with prospective employers, healthcare organisations, recruitment partners, authorised service providers or other organisations involved in the recruitment process.

We only share information reasonably necessary for recruitment, eligibility assessment, interview coordination, placement and related services.

4. Meta, Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram advertisement or Meta lead form, the information you provide may be transferred to IBREU Global Talent for recruitment and follow-up purposes.

Submission of a lead form does not guarantee employment, visa approval, interview selection or placement.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, organisational and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

6. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide recruitment services, maintain candidate records, comply with legal obligations and support future suitable employment opportunities.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. You may also ask us to stop using your information for certain purposes where legally applicable.

8. International Data Processing

As IBREU Global Talent supports international recruitment, candidate information may be processed or shared across countries where candidates, employers, recruitment partners or service providers are located.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website or advertisements may use third-party platforms such as Meta, Facebook, Instagram, hosting providers, analytics services and other technology providers. Their processing of information may also be governed by their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

IBREU Global Talent may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
